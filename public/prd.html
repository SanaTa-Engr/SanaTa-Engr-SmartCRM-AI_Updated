Executive Summary

SmartCRM AI is an intelligent, high-velocity customer relationship management platform engineered for modern B2B sales organizations, revenue operations (RevOps), account executives (AEs), and sales development reps (SDRs). Version 3.0 introduces real-time Google Maps Platform Lead Discovery & Interactive Spatial Intelligence alongside an overhauled, crystal-clear Sales Pipeline Funnel, bulk CSV/Excel ingestion, and Google Gemini AI automated scoring, instant profile dossiers, and conversational sales copilot capabilities.

1. Product Vision & Strategic Objectives

Traditional CRMs act as passive, retrospective digital filing cabinets that require extensive administrative data entry while providing little proactive guidance. SmartCRM AI re-architects the CRM into an active revenue acceleration engine that surfaces verified local and enterprise leads, scores them with predictive intelligence, and moves them across a transparent pipeline with minimal friction.

1.1 Core Value Drivers

  • Google Maps Geospatial Prospecting: Discovers live, verified local businesses and commercial prospects via the modern Google Places API (New) and renders them on an interactive Google Map with custom markers, instant InfoWindows, and 1-click CRM conversion.
  • Crystal-Clear Sales Funnel: Visualizes deal flow through an intuitive 5-step progression funnel (New Inbound → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Closed Won) with stage definitions, column value aggregates, and 1-click stage advancement buttons.
  • Predictive Lead Triage: Quantitatively evaluates prospects on a 0-100 scale using firmographics, budget tier, and intent signals, generating human-readable reasoning and prescriptive next steps.
  • High-Throughput File Ingestion: Ingests up to 10MB of CSV and Microsoft Excel data with automatic column mapping, duplicate detection, and audit history.
  • Zero-Friction Outreach: Generates multi-tone personalized sales outreach and executive meeting summaries in sub-second latency.

2. User Personas & Core Journeys

2.1 Field Sales & Territory Account Executives (AEs)

  • Primary Need: Discovering local businesses in target geographic territories, mapping prospect routes, qualifying commercial opportunities, and advancing deals quickly to close.
  • Key Workflows: Searching Google Maps for businesses in specific cities, reviewing ratings and website footprints, converting top prospects with 1-click into the CRM, and utilizing the clear 5-stage sales funnel to close contracts.

2.2 Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)

  • Primary Need: High-volume lead discovery, contact validation, automated triage, and personalized outbound follow-ups.
  • Key Workflows: Using Google Maps niche presets (e.g. Restaurants, Law Firms, Tech Agencies) to bulk-import 20+ leads at once, running AI Predictive Fit Scoring, and generating tailored cold emails.

2.3 VP of Sales & Revenue Operations (RevOps)

  • Primary Need: Pipeline accuracy, predictable revenue forecasting, duplicate prevention, and clean data hygiene.
  • Key Workflows: Monitoring total pipeline value across stages, reviewing conversion drop-offs in the funnel bar, and auditing imported lead sources.

4. Detailed Functional Requirements

4.1 Google Maps Lead Discovery & Spatial Intelligence

  • Official SDK Integration: Powered by @vis.gl/react-google-maps with APIProvider, Map (using DEMO_MAP_ID and internal usage attribution gmp_mcp_codeassist_v1_aistudio), and AdvancedMarker elements.
  • Modern Places API (New): Discovers commercial prospects using the https://places.googleapis.com/v1/places:searchText REST endpoint with strict field masking for efficiency and cost optimization.
  • Interactive Synchronization: Hovering or selecting any lead card in the sidebar highlights the corresponding pin on Google Maps with a glowing pulsing ring; clicking any marker pans the camera, opens a rich InfoWindow, and scrolls the card into view.
  • Visual Pin Status: Unsaved businesses feature high-contrast indigo pins; businesses already active in the CRM pipeline display emerald pins with verified checkmark glyphs.
  • Rich InfoWindow: Displays business name, category pill, star rating with review count, formatted physical address, direct phone link (tel:), website link, Google Maps navigation link, and 1-click "+ Add to Leads Pipeline" action.
  • Niche & Location Presets: Instant 1-click industry buttons (Restaurants, Cafes & Bakeries, Software & Tech, Real Estate, Dental Clinics, Law Firms, Retail Boutiques, Gyms & Fitness) and target city presets (San Francisco, Austin, New York, Chicago, Miami, Seattle).
  • Discovery Filters: Filter by Minimum Star Rating (4.0+, 4.5+, 4.8+), Has Phone Number, Has Website, and Unsaved Leads Only; sort by Top Rated, Most Reviews, or Company Name.
  • 3-Way View Layouts: Responsive toggle between Split View (sidebar list + interactive map), Map Only (fullscreen interactive map), and List Only.
  • Bulk Lead Ingestion: "Add All (X) to Pipeline" button allowing one-click batch import of all visible Google Maps prospects with automated deal value estimation and initial AI fit scores.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Prevents duplicate lead records by verifying both Google Maps placeId and normalized company name against existing CRM records.

4.2 Transparent Sales Pipeline & Lead Management

  • Interactive Pipeline Stepper Funnel: Top-level visual funnel banner showing the 5 progressive stages:
    Step 1: New Inbound → Step 2: Contacted → Step 3: Qualified → Step 4: Proposal Sent → Step 5: Closed Won (plus Closed Lost).
  • Clear Stage Definitions: Each Kanban column header explicitly defines its qualification criteria (e.g. "Budget, authority & interest verified") alongside total deal count and aggregate dollar volume.
  • 1-Click Stage Advancement: Prominent forward action buttons on each card (e.g., Advance to Contacted →, Advance to Qualified →, Send Proposal →, Mark Won ♥) and back-step arrow (←), removing friction from moving deals through the funnel.
  • Google Maps Origin Tagging: Leads discovered via Google Maps display a distinct • Google Maps pill, address preview, and direct link to open the live business listing on Google Maps.
  • Predictive AI Fit Scoring: Prospects are scored 0-100 with color-coded badges (High Fit 80-100 in green, Good Fit 60-79 in amber, Low Fit in slate) with natural-language reasoning and next-step recommendations.
  • Smart Sales Actions: Single-click triggers for AI Profile Dossier, AI Follow-Up Email Drafter, Lead Editing, and Deletion.
  • Dense Table View: Tabular representation with sortable columns, location links, deal values, and quick stage transition triggers.

4.3 Automated Bulk Lead Import Engine (CSV / XLSX)

  • File Ingestion: Native client/server parsing of CSV and Microsoft Excel (.xlsx/.xls) files up to 10MB.
  • Header Auto-Mapping: Automatic matching of incoming spreadsheet headers to CRM fields with interactive dropdown overrides.
  • Source Normalization: Enforces standard sales channels: Inbound Web, Outbound SDR, Referral, Partner Ecosystem, Conference, and Google Maps.
  • Real-Time Deduplication: Prevents duplicates across existing CRM databases and internal spreadsheet rows.
  • Interactive Preview: Color-coded breakdown of valid, duplicate, and invalid rows with descriptive error flags.
  • Audit Ledger: Persistent history tracking import dates, filenames, success counts, and error reports.

4.4 Deal Pipeline & Revenue Forecasting

  • Deal Stages: Discovery → Demo Scheduled → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Closed Won / Lost.
  • Weighted Revenue Forecasts: Calculates probability-adjusted pipeline totals for executive financial forecasting.
  • Deal Risk Analyzer: Flags stagnant deals and provides AI deal-winning recommendations.

4.5 AI Sales Copilot

  • Conversational Sidebar Copilot: Always-on AI assistant grounded in real-time pipeline metrics, deal statuses, and contact accounts.
  • Capabilities: Answering pipeline queries, drafting bespoke sales proposals, identifying at-risk deals, and coaching sales tactics.

5. Security, Data Privacy & Governance

  • Zero Client-Side Secret Exposure: Google Maps Platform API keys and Gemini API credentials are maintained on the backend proxy server and never exposed in client bundles.
  • Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforce strict per-user boundaries (auth.uid() = user_id).
  • Input Sanitization: Strict payload caps, MIME-type checks, and regex validation on all search and import endpoints.
